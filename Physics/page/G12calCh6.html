 (ii) The frequencies used in imaging are typically in the range of 1 to 15 MHz. What is the  range of wavelengths in human tissue where the velocity of sound is 1540 m s-1?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans: (i) The ultrasound is used in medical imaging rather than sound wave of audible frequencies or lower. Because ultrasound have higher frequency and shorter wavelength. Shorter wavelengths are more easily reflected or refracted in the superficial tissues than longer wavelengths. Thus, medical imaging used ultrasound gives better resolution.

(ii)

v = 1540 m s-1, \(\lambda \) = ?

f1 = 1 MHz = 1 \(\times \) \({10}^{6}\) Hz

f2 = 15 MHz = 15 \(\times \) \({10}^{6}\) Hz

=

f \(\lambda \)

 

\(\lambda \)

=

\(\frac{v}{f}\)

 

\({\lambda }_{1}\) = \(\frac{v}{{f}_{1}}\)

\({\lambda }_{2}\) = \(\frac{v}{{f}_{2}}\)

 

= \(\frac{1540}{1\,\times \,{10}^{6}}\)

= \(\frac{1540}{15\,\times \,{10}^{6}}\)

 

= 1.54 \(\times {10}^{-3}\) m

The range of wavelengths is between 0.103 \(\times {10}^{-3}\) m and 1.54 \(\times {10}^{-3}\) m.
