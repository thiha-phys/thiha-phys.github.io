Rev.3
Q:

Rev.3.   Why are the units of energy and work the same?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:   Energy is the capacity to do work. Whenever work is done on the body, the energy gained by the body is equal to the amount of work done on it. So, the units of energy and work are the same.
