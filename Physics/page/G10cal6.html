No.2.   (a) A liquid-in-glass clinical thermometer is marked with a scale from 32 C to 42 C which covers a distance of 80 mm. A liquid-in-glass laboratory thermometer is marked with a scale from 0 C to 100 C, which covers a distance of 160 mm. State and explain which thermometer; (i) has the greater range, (ii) is more sensitive.

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:

(i)   Laboratory thermometer has the greater range.

  The range of laboratory thermometer (0\(℃\) to 100 \(℃\) ) = 100 \(℃\)

  The range of clinical thermometer (32\(℃\) to 42 \(℃\) ) = 10 \(℃\)

(ii)   Clinical thermometer is more sensitive. (scale ပြောင်းလဲမှုကို သိလွယ်မြင်လွယ်သည်)

  Sensitivity of clinical thermometer = \(\frac{80 mm}{10 ℃}\) = 8 mm \({℃}^{-1}\)

  Sensitivity of laboratory thermometer = \(\frac{160 mm}{100 ℃}\) = 1.6 mm \({℃}^{-1}\)

Q:

No.6.     The coefficient of volume expansion of Pyrex glass is one-third that of ordinary glass. Which glass can stand more thermal strain?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:     Pyrex glass can stand more thermal strain than ordinary glass. Lower coefficient of volume expansion means less expansion and contraction with temperature changes. A beaker made of Pyrex glass will not break when boiling water is poured into it, while a tumbler made of ordinary glass is likely to crack.
