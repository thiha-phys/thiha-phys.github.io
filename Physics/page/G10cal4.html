Rev.1
Q:

Rev.1.   A person exerts pressure on the floor when standing, sitting and lying. Explain why the pressure is different when the person is in each of these positions.

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:   According to p = \(\frac{F}{A}\) ,     p \(∝\) \(\frac{1}{A}\) (force = weight is constant for all positon)

  The pressure is different for different contact areas.

  The contact area in standing is smallest and the pressure is largest.

  The contact area in lying is largest and the pressure is smallest.

No.6
Q:

No.6.     A mini-submarine has the total volume of 24 m3. Its mass is 2 000 kg. Can it carry another 3 000 kg?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:

\(ρ\) = 1000 kg m-3,   V = 24 m3,   msub = 2000 kg,   mload = 3000 kgs

Maximum weight of water displaced, w

= m g

= \(ρ\) V g

= 1 000 × 24 × 10 = 240 000 N

By Archimedes' principle,

Upward thrust = weight of water displaced = 240 000 N

Total mass of submarine and load, m

= msub + mload

= 2 000 + 3 000 = 5 000 kg

Total weight of submarine and load, w

= m g

= 5 000 × 10 = 50 000 N

Since, upward thrust > total weight of submarine and load, it can carry another 3000 kg.

For iron

m iron = 80 g
