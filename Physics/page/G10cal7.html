No.10
Q:

No.10.   Define wavelength, frequency and velocity of a sound wave. Write down the relationship between them. Can this relationship be used for other waves (such as light waves)?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:     Wavelength ()

    The distance between any two consecutive wave crests (or) any two consecutive wave troughs is called the wavelength.

    Frequency (f)

    The number of complete waves passing a point per second is called the frequency of the waves.

    Velocity of a sound Wave (v)

    The velocity of wave is the speed with which a wave crest travels.

    The relationship between wavelength (\(λ\)), frequency (f), velocity (v) of a sound wave is v = f \(λ\)

    Yes, it can be used for other periodic waves.

No.12
Q:

No.12.   What is meant by (a) reflection (b) refraction and (c) diffraction?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:     Reflection of waves

    When a series of waves strike an obstacle, they are turned back. This turning back of waves is called reflection of waves.

    Refraction of waves

    When waves are incident to the boundary with an angle, the direction of the waves changes. Such a change in direction is called refraction.

    Diffraction of waves

    Diffraction is the spreading of waves from the straight-on direction through a gap (or) moves around an obstacle.
