Q:

Eg.2.   A soccer ball is kicked at an angle with the ground. The ball traverses the horizontal distance of 10 m and the vertical distance of 3 m in 3 s. Find the displacement and the average velocity of the ball in 3 s.

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:

\(∆x\) = 10 m, \(∆y\) = 3 m, t = 3 s,

\(∆\)r = ? \(\bar{v}\) = ?

Magnitude of the displacement,

\({(∆r)}^{2}\)

= \({(∆x)}^{2}+{(∆y)}^{2}\)

\(∆\)r

= \(\sqrt{{(∆x)}^{2}+{(∆y)}^{2}}\)

= \(\sqrt{{(10)}^{2}+{(3)}^{2}}\)

= \(\sqrt{109}\) = 10.44 m

Direction of the displacement,

tan \(θ\)

= \(\frac{∆y}{∆x}\)

\(θ\)

= tan-1 \(\frac{∆y}{∆x}\)

\(θ\)

= tan-1 \(\frac{3}{10}\) = 16.7\(°\)

Magnitude of the average velocity,

\(\bar{v}\)

= \(\frac{∆r}{∆t}\)

= \(\frac{10.44}{3}\) = 3.48 m s-1

The direction of the displacement and the average velocity are the same which makes an angle 16.7\(°\) with the ground.

Rev.2
Q:

Rev.2.   Why is the horizontal motion of the projectile taken as uniform motion?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:   When the air resistance is neglected, there is no horizontal force on the projectile. The horizontal component of the velocity of the projectile is constant, (v0x = vx). Thus, the horizontal motion of the projectile is taken as uniform motion.

Rev.3
Q:

Rev.3.   Under what conditions can you have the two-dimensional motion with a one-dimensional acceleration?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:   When the air resistance is neglected and the only gravitational force is acting on an object, we can have the two-dimensional motion with a one-dimensional acceleration.

position (x,y) = ?   v = ?

= v0 cos \({θ}_{0}\)

= 141.4 cos 45\(°\) = 99.98 m s-1

v0y

= v0 sin \({θ}_{0}\)

= 141.4 sin 45\(°\) = 99.98 m s-1

x

= v0x t + \(\frac{1}{2}\) \(a\)x t2

= 99.98 × 5 = 499.9 m

y

= v0y t + \(\frac{1}{2}\) \(a\)y t2

= 99.98 × 5 \(-\) \(\frac{1}{2}\) × 9.8 × 52

= 377.4 m

vx

= v0x + \(a\)x t

= 99.98 m s-1

= v0y + \(a\)y t

= 99.98 \(–\) (9.8 × 5)

= 50.98 m s-1

= \({v}_{x}^{2}\) + \({v}_{y}^{2}\)

= \(\sqrt{{v}_{x}^{2} + {v}_{y}^{2}}\)

= 112.27 m s-1

= \(\frac{{v}_{y}}{{v}_{x}}\)

Eg.4.   A bomb is dropped from an airplane moving horizontally with its speed of 50 m s-1. If the bomb will reach the ground in 5 s, find the altitude of the plane. The air resistance is negligible.

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:

v0 = 50 m s-1, t = 5 s, y = ?

\(a\)x = 0, \(a\)y = \(-\)g, \({θ}_{0}\) = 0°

v0x = 50 m s-1, v0y = 0 m s-1

y

= v0y t + \(\frac{1}{2}\) \(a\)y t2

= v0y t \(-\) \(\frac{1}{2}\) g t2

= \(-\) \(\frac{1}{2}\) × 9.8 × 52 = \(-\) 122.5 m

Minus sign means that the direction of y component displacement is downwards. The altitude of the plane is 122.5 m.
