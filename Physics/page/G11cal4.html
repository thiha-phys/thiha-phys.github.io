Rev.1
Q:

Rev.1.   Two students climb up the stairs of a building and reach the top at the same time. Their weights are different. Which student will expand more power?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:   Since P = \(\frac{W}{t}\) = \(\frac{m g h}{t}\) = \(\frac{w h}{t}\) (\(∵\)w = mg)

  h and t are constant.

  Power is directly proportional to the weight. Therefore, the student having a greater weight will expend more power.

Rev.2
Q:

Rev.2.   Which is more advantageous: to pay wages according to the amount of work done (or) according the power?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:   To pay wages according to the amount of work done is more advantage.

  Since P = \(\frac{W}{t}\) , W = P t, if power is constant, W \(∝\) t

  For the same power the amount of work done (W) is large when the working time (t) is large and the amount of work done (W) is small when the working time (t) is small.

Rev.5
Q:

Rev.5.   What are renewable and non-renewable energy?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:   Renewable energy is the energy from sources that will not run out or will be replenished for thousands or even millions of years. The renewable energy is safe to the environment.

  Non-renewable energy is the energy from sources that will run out or will not be replenished for thousands or even millions of years. The non-renewable energy is not safe to the environment.
