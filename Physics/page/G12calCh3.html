Q:  Derive expression for terminal velocity when a ball of radius r is dropped through a liquid of viscosity η and density ρ.

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans: The forces acting on the object are;

 -weight of the sphere ball,  

  w = mg = ρ V g = \(\frac{4}{3}\) π r3 ρ1 g

 -upward thrust, F1 = mg = ρ V g = \(\frac{4}{3}\) π r3 ρ2 g

 -drag force, F = 6 π η r v

 At a constant velocity of the fall (terminal velocity), the upward and downward forces are in balance.

  F1 + F = w

 \(\frac{4}{3}\) π r3 ρ2 g + 6 π η r v = \(\frac{4}{3}\) π r3 ρ1 g

  6 π η r v = \(\frac{4}{3}\) π r3 ρ1 g - \(\frac{4}{3}\) π r3 ρ2 g

  6 π η r v = \(\frac{4}{3}\) π r3 (ρ1 - ρ2) g

  v = \(\frac{4}{3}\times \) \(\frac{\,{r}^{3}\,({\rho }_{1}\,-\,{\rho }_{2})\,g}{6\,\,\,r}\)

  v = \(\frac{2\,\,g\,{r}^{2}\,({\rho }_{1}\,-\,{\rho }_{2})}{9\,}\)

 Where, η = viscosity of the fluid,

  g = the acceleration due to gravity,

  ρ1 = the density of the sphere,

  ρ2 = the density of the liquid,

  r = radius of the falling sphere,

  v = terminal velocity of sphere.
