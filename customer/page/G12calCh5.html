Eg.1

Q:  A person walking at a regular speed generations heat at the rate of 0.07 W. If the surface area of the body is 1.5 m2 and heat is to be generated 0.03 m below the skin, what should be the temperature difference between the skin and interior of the body if the heat is to be conducted to the surface of the skin? (\(k\) = 5 \(\times \) 10-5 W m-1 K-1)

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:

H = 0.07 W, A = 1.5 m2, l = 0.03 m

T2 – T1 = ?, \(k\) = 5 \(\times \) 10-5 W m-1 K-1

 

By rate of heat conduction,

 

Hcond

=

\(k\) A \(\frac{{T}_{2}\,-\,{T}_{1}}{l}\)

 

T2 – T1

=

\(\frac{{H}_{\mathrm{cond}\,}l}{k\,A}\)

 

 

=

\(\frac{0.07\times 0.03\,}{5\,\times \,1{0}^{-5}\,\times 1.5}\)

 

 

=

28 K (or) 28 \(℃\)

Q:  The area and thickness of a glass plate of a window are 0.25 m2 and 4 mm respectively. The temperature of inside surface of glass plate is 25 \(℃\) and its outside temperature is 26\(℃\). Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

A = 0.25 m2, l = 4 mm = 4 \(\times \) 10-3 m

T2 = 26 \(℃\) = 26 + 273 = 299 K

T1 = 25 \(℃\) = 25 + 273 = 298 K

\(k\) = 0.78 W m-1 K-1, \(∆\)Q = ?

t = 1 h = 3600 s

 

By rate of heat conduction,

 

Hcond

=

\(k\) A \(\frac{{T}_{2}\,-\,{T}_{1}}{l}\)

 

Hcond

=

\(\frac{∆Q}{t}\)

 

\(\frac{∆Q}{t}\)

=

\(k\) A \(\frac{{T}_{2}\,-\,{T}_{1}}{l}\)

 

\(∆\)Q

=

\(k\) A \(\frac{{T}_{2}\,-\,{T}_{1}}{l}\) t

 

 

=

0.78 \(\times \) 0.25 \(\frac{(299\,-\,298\,)}{4\,\times \,1{0}^{-3}}\) \(\times \) 3600

 

 

=

175 500 J

Eg.2

Q:  A new computer chip with a surface area of 1 cm2 generate 10 W of heat. Determine the convective heat transfer coefficient of the material needed to keep the temperature of the chip less than 20 \(℃\) above the environmental temperature. Can the chip be cooled with air, or will it require forced convection? Convective heat transfer coefficient of air is 2.5 to 25 W m-2 K-1.

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:

A = 1 cm2 = 1 (10-2 m)2 = 1 \(\times \) 10-4 m2,

H = -10 W (generate)

T2 – T1 = - 20 \(℃\) = - 20 K (T1 > T2)

q = ?

 

By rate of heat convection,

 

Hconv

=

q A (T2 – T1)

 

q

=

\(\frac{{H}_{\mathrm{conv}}}{A\,({T}_{2}-\,{T}_{1})}\)

 

 

=

\(\frac{-\,10}{1\,\times 1{0}^{-4}\,(-20)}\,\)

 

 

=

5000 W m-2 K-1

 

Since convective heat transfer coefficient of air is very much less than 5000 W m-2 K-1, the chip cannot be cooled with air. Therefore, it will require the forced convection.

Q:  In a room, the temperature of water in a kettle is 80 \(℃\). The room temperature is 30 \(℃\) and the surface area of the kittle is 0.02 m2. If the emissivity is 0.9. Find the rate of heat loss due to radiation. (\(\sigma \) = 5.685 \(\times \) 10-8 W m-2 K-4)

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:

T2 = 80 \(℃\) = 80 + 273 = 353 K

T1 = 30 \(℃\) = 30 + 273 = 303 K

A = 0.02 m2, e = 0.9

H = ? \(\sigma \) = 5.685 \(\times \) 10-8 W m-2 K-4

 

By rate of heat radiation,

 

Hrad

=

e \(\sigma \) A T4

 

Hout

=

e \(\sigma \) A \({T}_{2}^{4}\)

 

Hin

=

e \(\sigma \) A \({T}_{1}^{4}\)

 

Hlost

=

Hout - Hin

 

 

=

e \(\sigma \) A \({T}_{2}^{4}\) - e \(\sigma \) A \({T}_{1}^{4}\)

 

 

=

e \(\sigma \) A (\({T}_{2}^{4}\) - \({T}_{1}^{4}\))

 

 

=

0.9\(\times \)5.685\(\times \)10-8\(\times \)0.02(\({353}^{4}\) - \({303}^{4}\))

 

 

=

7.26 W
