Rev.3
Q:

Rev.3.   Define average velocity and instantaneous velocity.

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:     The average velocity is the ratio of the total displacement \(\vec{s}\) to the time taken t.

    average velocity, \({\vec{v}}_{av}\) = \(\frac{\vec{s}}{t}\)     (or)   \(\bar{\vec{v}}\) = \(\frac{\vec{s}}{t}\)

    The instantaneous velocity is defined as the time rate of change of displacement.

    \(\vec{v}\) = \(\frac{lim}{∆t →0} \frac{∆ \vec{s} }{∆ t }\) = \(\frac{d\vec{s}}{dt}\)

  The instantaneous velocity is defined as the time rate of change of displacement.   Velocity is a vector quantity.

Is the magnitude of the average velocity the same as the value of the average speed? (Hint: For the innermost lane, the starting point is the same as the finishing point.)

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

    total distance, s = 400 m,     t = 50 s

    Average speed, \({v}_{av}\) = \(\frac{s}{t}\) = \(\frac{400}{50}\) = 8 m s-1

    So, the magnitude of the average velocity is not the same as the value of average speed.
