No.1
Q:

No.1.   (a)   What is a lens? State some of its uses.

  (b)   What do you understand by focus of a convex lens and focus of a concave lens?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:   (a)   A transparent material which can diverge or converge rays of light is called a lens. A lens has at least one curved surface.

  (b)   The rays parallel to the principal axis converge at a point on the principal axis after refraction through a convex lens. This point is called the principal focus of the convex lens.

    The rays parallel to the principal axis are divergent after refraction through a concave lens. Those divergent rays appear to come from a point on the principal axis. This point is called the principal focus of the concave lens.

No.4.   State sign convection for lenses.

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:   Distances of real object, real image and real focus are positive. Distances of virtual object, virtual image and virtual focus are negative.

  The perpendicular distance measured above the principal axis is positive and that below the principal axis is negative.

  Focal length of convex lens is positive. Focal length of concave lens is negative.

Q:

Rev.3.   What are the differences between real and virtual images formed by a convex lens?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:

Real

Images formed by the rays of light that actually pass through it.

It is on the opposite side of the object.

It can be formed on a screen.

It is inverted.

Virtual

Image from the rays of light appear to come but do not actually pas through it.

It is on the same side of the object.

It cannot be formed on a screen.

It is erect.
