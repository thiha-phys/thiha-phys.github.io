Eg.1
Q:

Eg.1.   The magnitude of \(\vec{A}\) and \(\vec{B}\) are 5 units and 8 units respectively. If the angle between \(\vec{A}\) and \(\vec{B}\) is 60, calculate

  (i) \(\vec{A}\) ⋅ \(\vec{B}\) (ii) \(\vec{A}\) \(×\) \(\vec{B}\).

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:

\(\vec{A}\) = 5 units, \(\vec{B}\) = 8 units,   \(θ\) = 60

(i)

\(\vec{A}\) ⋅ \(\vec{B}\)

= A B cos \(θ\)

= 5 × 8 cos 60

= 20 units

(ii)

\(\vec{A}\) \(×\) \(\vec{B}\)

= A B sin \(θ\)

= 5 × 8 sin 60

= 34.64 units

The direction of \(\vec{A}\) \(×\) \(\vec{B}\) is perpendicular to both \(\vec{A}\) and \(\vec{B}\).
