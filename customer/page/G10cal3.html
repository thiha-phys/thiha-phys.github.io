Rev.1
Q:

Rev.1.   Is it correct to describe Newton's second law in symbols as \(\vec{F}\) \(∝\) \(\vec{a}\)?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:   Yes. By Newton’s second law of motion, \(\vec{F}\) = m \(\vec{a}\).

  m = constant,   \(\vec{F}\) \(∝\) \(\vec{a}\)     \(\vec{F}\) = net force,   \(\vec{a}\) = acceleration

Rev.2
Q:

Rev.2.   Although two forces act simultaneously on a body, it continues to move with a constant velocity. What can be said about the two forces?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:   When the body moves with a constant velocity, it has no acceleration. According to Newton's second law, the net force on the body will be zero. (F = m a)

  So, two forces acting simultaneously on that body are equal in magnitude and opposite in directions. (\(\vec{{F}_{1}}\) = - \(\vec{{F}_{2}}\))

Rev.3
Q:

Rev.3.   Can action and reaction cancel each other? Why?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:   No, action and reaction cannot cancel each other since action-reaction pair acts on two separate objects.

Rev.5
Q:

Rev.5.   The weight of a body may change when its location is changed, but mass does not. Why?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:   Mass is the quantity of matter in a body. Mass does not dependent on the gravity. Thus, mass does not change although its location changes.

  Weight is the force of gravity and gravity changes with location. Thus, the weight of a body may change when its location is changed.
