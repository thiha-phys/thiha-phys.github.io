Rev.1
Q:

Rev.1.   Explain each of the following terms in your own words:

    physical quantity, basic unit and derived unit.

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:     Physical quantity

    A physical quantity is the quantity that can be measured, and consists of a numerical magnitude and a unit.

    Q = N u,   Where, Q = physical quantity, N = a dimensionless number, u = unit.

    Basic unit

    A basic unit is a unit of basic type physical quantities such as length, mass, time, temperature, electric current, amount of substance, luminous intensity.

    Derived unit

    A derived unit is a unit of measurement formed by combining the basic (or base) units of a system.

No.2
Q:

No.2.   Determine the derived units of:

  (a) speed (= distance/ time)

  (b) volume (= length \(×\) width \(×\) height)

  (c) density (= mass/ volume)

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:     (a)   speed = \(\frac{distance}{time}\)

      unit   \(⟹\)   speed = \(\frac{m}{s}\) = m s-1 (metre per second)

    (b)   volume = length × width × height

      unit   \(⟹\)   volume = m × m × m = m3 (cubic metre)

    (c)   density = \(\frac{mass}{volume}\)

      unit   \(⟹\)   density = \(\frac{kg}{{m}^{3}}\) = kg m-3 (kilogram per cubic metre)

Rev.3
Q:

Rev.3.   In each of the following pairs, which quantity is larger?

    (a) 2 km (or) 2500 m,   (b) 2 m (or) 1500 mm,     (c) 2000 g (or) 3 kg

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:     (a) 2 km (or) 2500 m

      2 km = 2 \(×{10}^{3}\) m = 2000 m       \(∴\) 2500 m is larger.

    (b) 2 m (or) 1500 mm

      1500 mm = 1500 \(×{10}^{-3}\) m = 1.5 m     \(∴\) 2 m is larger.

    (c) 2000 g (or) 3 kg

      3 kg = 3 \(×{10}^{3}\) g = 3000 g       \(∴\) 3 kg is larger.

Q:

No.15.   According to observations and measurements the farthest galaxies are at a distance of 1026 m from the earth. When space travel becomes highly advanced, do you think that man will be able to visit those galaxies? (Light travels 3 \(×\) 108 m in one second and there are approximately 108 s in a year.)

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:

Light travels 3 \(×\) 108 m = 1 s,     1 year = 108 s

Distance

=

1026 m

Time taken

=

1026 m \(×\frac{1 s}{3 × {10}^{8} m}\)

=

0.333 \(×{10}^{18}\) s

=

0.333 \(×{10}^{18}\) s \(×\frac{1 year}{{10}^{8} s}\)

=

0.333 \(×{10}^{10}\) years (or) 3.33 \(×{10}^{9}\) years

Age of man

\(~\)

100 years = 102 years

Since age of man is very less than the time take to reach galaxies, man will not be able to visit those galaxies.
