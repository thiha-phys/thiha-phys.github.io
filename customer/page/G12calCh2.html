Ans:

Mass, m = 8.3 kg, Tmax = 1 500 N, 

r = 80 cm = 80 \(\times {10}^{-2}\) m, g = 9.8 m s-2

maximum speed, vmax = ?

 

For horizontal circle,

 

FC

=

T

 

\(\frac{m\,{v}_{\max}^{2}}{r}\)

=

Tmax

 

\(\frac{8.3\,\,{v}_{\max}^{2}}{80\,\times \,{10}^{-2}}\)

=

1500

 

\({v}_{\max}^{2}\)

=

\(\frac{1500\,\times \,80\,\times \,{10}^{-2}}{8.3}\)

 

vmax

=

\(\sqrt{\frac{1500\,\times \,80\,\times \,{10}^{-2}}{8.3}}\)

 

 

=

12.02 m s-1

Ans:

m = 3 kg, g = 9.8 m s-2

r = 60 cm = 60 \(\times \,{10}^{-2}\)m, v = ?

Force acting on bucket are

Tension force, T = 50 N

Weight, w = mg = 3 \(\times \,\)9.8 = 29.4 N

 

For vertical circle, at lowest point,

Fnet = T – w = 50 - 29.4 = 20.6 N

 

(Since Fnet provides the centripetal acceleration)

 

FC

=

Fnet

 

\(\frac{m\,{v}^{2}}{r}\)

=

Fnet

 

\(\frac{3\,{v}^{2}}{60\,\times \,{10}^{-2}}\)

=

20.6

 

v2

=

20.6 \(\times {20\times 10}^{-2}\)

 

v

=

\(\sqrt{20.6\times {20\times 10}^{-2}}\)

 

 

=

2.03 m s-1

(i) v = ?, (ii) T = ?

(i)

For vertical circle, At the top position,

Fnet = T + w = T + mg

 

(Since Fnet provides the centripetal acceleration)

 

At the top, For minimum speed, Tmin = 0

 

FC

=

Fnet

 

\(\frac{m\,{v}^{2}}{r}\)

=

T + mg

 

\(\frac{m\,{v}_{\min}^{2}}{r}\)

=

m g

 

\(\frac{\,{v}_{\min}^{2}}{r}\)

=

g

 

\({v}_{\min}^{2}\)

=

g r

 

vmin

=

\(\sqrt{g\,r}\) = \(\sqrt{9.8\,\times \,1.1}\)

 

 

=

3.283 m s-1

(ii)

At the bottom, the speed is twice than at the top.

 

v = 2 vmin = 2 \(\times \) 3.283 = 6.566 m s-1

 

At the bottom, \({F}_{C}\) = T - w

Q:  An athlete weighing 790 N is running a curve at a speed of 6 m s-1 in an arc. The radius of curvature of the arc is 5 m. Find the centripetal force acting on him.

 Which force provides the centripetal force?

 What will happen to him if the radius of curvature is smaller?

Ans: အဖြေနှင့် တွက်နည်းကြည့်ရန် နှိပ်ပါ

Ans:

w = 790 N, v = 6 m s-1, r = 5 m,

g = 9.8 m s-2, FC = ?

m = \(\frac{w}{g}\) = \(\frac{790}{9.8}\) = 80.61 kg

 

FC

=

\(\frac{m\,{v}^{2}}{r}\) = \(\frac{80.61\,(6\,{)}^{2}}{5}\) = 580.4 N

\(⇒\)

\(⇒\)

Frictional force between the feet and ground provides the centripetal force.

If the radius of curvature is smaller, the centripetal force will become greater. He will slip since the frictional force is less than the required centripetal force. [Ff < FC, he will slip]
