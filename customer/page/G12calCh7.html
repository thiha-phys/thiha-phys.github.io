h = 6.62 \(\times \,{10}^{-34}\) J s, c = 3 \(\times \,{10}^{8}\) m s-1

number of photon per second = ?

Output energy in 1 s is \({10}^{-3}\) J.

 

Energy of a photon,

 

 E

=

\(\frac{h\,c}{\lambda }\)

 

 

=

\(\frac{6.62\,\times \,{10}^{-34}\,\times \,3\,\times \,{10}^{8}}{6328\,\times \,{10}^{-10}}\)

 

 

=

3.14 \(\times \,{10}^{-19}\) J

 

Number of photons per second

 

 = \(\frac{\mathrm{Output}\,\mathrm{energy}\,\mathrm{per}\,\mathrm{second}}{\mathrm{Energy}\,\mathrm{of}\,a\,\mathrm{photon}}\)

 

 = \(\frac{1\,\times \,{10}^{-3}}{3.14\,\times \,{10}^{-19}}\) = 3.18 \(\times \,{10}^{15}\) photons
